Card Companion

Prototype. A practice partner for picture-card communication (PECS) that a therapist sets up. The child holds a card up to the camera and the companion responds.

How it works

What it uses

  • A small image recognizer that learns each child's own cards from a few seconds of video.
  • Hand-designed behavior, the same kind of character AI used in games, so every response is predictable and reviewable.
  • No chatbot or language model. Nothing it says is generated.

What it will never do

  • Say anything outside the Responses list. That list is everything it can say.
  • Go online. The vision model downloads once during setup, then it runs offline.
  • Store or send video. Recognition happens on this device.
  • Prompt or pressure the child. It waits for the child to start.
  • Ignore "no." "No" stops everything instantly, every time.

Who it's for

  • Extra practice of skills a therapist has already taught, between sessions.
  • The therapist sets it up and reads the session summary. A grown-up brings real items. It supports people; it does not replace them.
